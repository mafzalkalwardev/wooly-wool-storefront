Return Policy

Important return/refund rules for handmade and custom products.

Overview

Wooly Wool sells handmade products and custom embroidery items. Due to the nature of handmade work, return policy may vary depending on product type.

Custom Orders

Custom embroidery / personalized products are made on demand. Therefore, these items are non-returnable and non-refundable once order is confirmed.

Damaged / Wrong Item

If you receive a damaged product or wrong item, contact us within 24 hours with unboxing video/photos. We will review and resolve the issue.

Proof Required

  • Unboxing video is required for claims.
  • Product must be unused and in original condition.
  • Claims without proof may not be accepted.
