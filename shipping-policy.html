Shipping Policy

Delivery information, timelines and important shipping notes for Wooly Wool orders.

Delivery Coverage

Wooly Wool currently delivers in Pakistan only. We do not offer international shipping.

Processing Time

Handmade and custom embroidery orders require time to prepare. Depending on order type, processing can take 2–5 working days.

Delivery Timeline

Standard delivery time is usually 2–3 weeks after dispatch (may vary by city).

Important Notes

  • Please provide complete and correct delivery address at checkout.
  • Delivery delays may occur due to weather, courier issues, or high order volume.
  • For custom orders, dispatch date may change depending on design complexity.
  • COD orders should be received by the customer personally for smooth delivery.

Address Changes

If you need to change your address after placing the order, contact us immediately on WhatsApp. Address updates may not be possible after dispatch.

Need help with shipping?
Contact us on WhatsApp for dispatch confirmation and delivery updates.
