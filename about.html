About Wooly Wool

Wooly Wool is a handmade brand delivering premium wool items and custom embroidery — crafted with detail, love, and quality finishing.

Our Story

Wooly Wool was created with one goal: to provide premium handmade wool products and embroidery work that feels personal, unique, and long-lasting. Every order is carefully crafted with the same attention we give to our own family pieces.
